    How do I make the .zip?
    1. Find your recording in Finder (it ends in .screenstudio).
    2. Right-click it and choose Compress.
    3. Drop the resulting .zip above.

    Your recording is a folder that macOS shows as a single file, so it must be zipped before uploading. Files are deleted from the server automatically.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert a ScreenStudio recording to MP4?

    Find your .screenstudio recording in Finder, right-click and choose Compress to make a .zip, then drop the .zip above and press Convert to MP4.

    Is FrameDrop really free?

    Yes. FrameDrop is 100% free and open source under the MIT license. There is no sign up and no watermark.

    Is my recording kept private?

    Your recording is processed on the server and the files are deleted automatically after conversion.

    Why do I need to zip the recording?

    A .screenstudio recording is actually a folder that macOS shows as one file. Compressing it to a .zip keeps every part of the recording together for upload.
