    How do I make the .zip?
    1. Find your recording in Finder (it ends in .screenstudio).
    2. Right-click it and choose Compress.
    3. Drop the resulting .zip above.

    Your recording is a folder that macOS shows as a single file, so it must be zipped before uploading. Files are deleted from the server automatically.
